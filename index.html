Quick answers

Before you book.

Is my appointment confirmed right away?

No. This form sends a booking request. Your appointment remains pending until the business confirms it.

Can I choose a service later?

Please choose the service that best fits your needs when submitting the form. You can mention extra details in the notes.

Are these prices final?

No. Prices are sample starting prices for this demo website and should be replaced with the business's actual prices before launch.

What happens to my request?

Your request is saved in the booking database so the business can review the details needed to arrange the appointment.
